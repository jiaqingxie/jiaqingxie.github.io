About Me

I am a first-year PhD student in the School of Computer Science at Fudan University. My research develops learning methods grounded in graph structure and spectral theory, with the goal of accelerating graph optimization and scientific discovery.

Research Interests

  • AI-Accelerated Advanced Graph Optimization
  • Spectral Graph Theory
  • Graph Algorithms for Science with AI
