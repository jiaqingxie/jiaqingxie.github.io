RxnPFN: Towards Universal Training-free Prediction of Reaction Properties from Enantioselectivity to Yield

Jiaqing Xie, Yuxin Wang, Xiaopeng He, Ben Gao, Zihao Ye, Yuqiang Li, Tong Zhu, Zheng Ling, Xipeng Qiu

Preprint, 2026.

tl;dr RxnPFN encodes multi-component reactions by chemical roles and bonds broken and formed, pairing a frozen pretrained tabular prior-fitted network with magnitude–direction decomposition for in-context reaction prediction in seconds without weight updates. Across 15,226 published asymmetric reactions it reaches pooled test R² = 0.856 (96.9% enantiomer sign accuracy on olefin hydrogenations; 90% without product stereochemistry), cuts prediction error on a withheld study by two thirds using four context measurements, and generalizes directly to ketone-reduction conversion, oxidation yield, cobalt hydrogenation, Sadphos Heck selectivity, and Buchwald–Hartwig yield.

VibraTrans: Cross-modal Translation between Infrared and Raman Spectra with Molecular Representation Learning

Jiaqing Xie, Zhuo Yang, Ben Gao, Shuaike Shen, Tianfan Fu, Yuqiang Li

Under Review at Nature Computational Science, 2026. Code

tl;dr VibraTrans is a source-conditioned diffusion Transformer that predicts either infrared or Raman vibrational spectra from the other in a single network evaluation using only the observed spectrum. Across QM9S, QMe14S, and ViBench-Full it exceeds Transformer and convolutional baselines in all six dataset–direction comparisons (mean per-spectrum R² 0.714–0.949) and raises structure-free IR-based logP and TPSA prediction R² to 0.800 and 0.908.

Deciphering Molecular Language for Spectral Interpretation with a Foundation Model Pretrained on Repository-Wide Metabolomics Data

Yifan Li, Yunhua Zhong, Pan Liu, Zikun Wang, Jiaqing Xie, Hongxin Xiang, Zihan Chen, Yuqiang Li, Jun Xia

Under Review at Nature Biotechnology, 2026. Code

tl;dr UltraMS is the first dual-polarity foundation model pretrained on over 160 million positive- and negative-ion-mode tandem mass spectra from GNPS and MassIVE via self-supervised learning on unannotated data, advancing molecular identification, peak-by-peak substructural attribution, property profiling, and clinical metabolomics discovery via UltraAtlas.

SpecMol: A Spectroscopy-Grounded Foundation Model for Multi-Task Molecular Learning

Shuaike Shen†, Jiaqing Xie†, Zhuo Yang, Antong Zhang, Shuzhou Sun, Ben Gao, Tianfan Fu, Biqing Qi, Yuqiang Li

Provisionally Accepted at Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling, 2026. arXiv

tl;dr SpecMol unifies continuous experimental spectroscopy (1H/13C NMR, IR, and MS), SMILES strings, and 3D molecular conformations in a single language-model interface via numerically faithful spectral serialization, accompanied by SpecMol-Bench covering structure elucidation, spectral simulation, and 3D conformation generation.

GSF-χ: Global Stereochemical Fields for Chiral Graph Transformers

Jiaqing Xie, Yuxin Wang, Xipeng Qiu

arXiv preprint, 2026. arXiv · Code

tl;dr GSF-χ lets every stereogenic unit modulate all pairwise attention through a reflection-even phase field while a handedness pseudoscalar χ reverses Chiral-RoPE on latent query–key blocks, leading every central-ECD output and improving axial Rotation and Symbol by 12.6% and 7.9% over the strongest baseline.

Analytic-Walk Rotary Positional Encodings for Graphs

Jiaqing Xie, Yuxin Wang, Xipeng Qiu

arXiv preprint, 2026. arXiv · Code

tl;dr AW-RoPE places rotary factors on edges and sums transported features over all walks via a differentiable linear solve or sparse recurrence, reducing nRMSE by 15–58% across five synthetic tasks and achieving the best mean on every real benchmark with Performer kernels and 12/13 datasets with GIN.

NMRTrans: Structure Elucidation from Experimental NMR Spectra via Set Transformers

Liujia Yang†, Zhuo Yang†, Jiaqing Xie†, Yubin Wang†, Ben Gao, Tianfan Fu, Xingjian Wei, Jiaxing Sun, Jiang Wu, Conghui He, Yuqiang Li, Qinying Gu

KDD, 2026. Paper · Code

tl;dr We build NMRSpec, a large-scale corpus of experimental 1H and 13C spectra mined from chemical literature, and propose NMRTrans, modeling spectra as unordered peak sets and improving Top-10 Accuracy over the strongest baseline by +17.82 points (61.15% vs. 43.33%).

R²-Seg: Training-Free OOD Medical Tumor Segmentation via Anatomical Reasoning and Statistical Rejection

Shuaike Shen, Ke Liu, Jiaqing Xie, Shangde Gao, Chunhua Shen, Ge Liu, Mireia Crispin-Ortuzar, Shangqi Gao

CVPR, 2026 (Oral). Paper · Code

tl;dr A training-free Reason-and-Reject framework combining LLM-guided anatomical ROI planning with two-sample statistical testing over BiomedParse predictions, substantially improving OOD tumor segmentation Dice, specificity, and sensitivity without parameter updates.

MatVerse: A Foundation Model for Materials Science

Xuanyu Zhu†, Qian Tan†, Bo Chen, Jiaqing Xie, Yang Han, Mei Wu, Xuanze Lin, Wanhao Liu, Yuchen Zhou, Shijie Wang, Biqing Qi, Shixiang Tang, Wenwei Zhang, Tianfan Fu, Kai Yu, Lu Chen, Yuqiang Li, Xin Chen

Technical Report, 2026.

tl;dr A materials foundation model built on Intern-S2-Preview (35B-A3B) via large-scale materials-domain post-training on ~150K multimodal examples, outperforming GPT-5.2 on MatSciBench (63.59% vs. 59.6%).

Disagree to Accelerate: Closing the Loop on Diffusion Feature Forecasts

Yanchao Li, Jiaqing Xie, Ben Gao, Wanhao Liu, Yanbo Wang, T. Y. Tsui, Jinfei Liu, Yuqiang Li, Tianfan Fu

arXiv preprint, 2026. arXiv · Code

tl;dr RACER turns disagreement between two cached feature forecasts into a free runtime reliability signal for a training-free closed-loop controller, improving diffusion acceleration across SD3.5-Large, FLUX.1-dev, Wan2.1-14B, and HunyuanVideo at equal denoiser evaluations.
