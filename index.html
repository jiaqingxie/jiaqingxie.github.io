Representative Papers
GSF-χ: Global Stereochemical Fields for Chiral Graph Transformers
Jiaqing Xie, Yuxin Wang†, Xipeng Qiu†
arXiv preprint, 2026
arXiv / Code

TL;DR: Enantiomers share atoms, bonds, and pairwise distances yet behave differently in chiral environments, so molecular encoders must respect rotations without going blind to reflection. GSF-χ lets every stereogenic unit modulate all pairwise attention through a reflection-even phase field, while a handedness pseudoscalar χ reverses a Chiral-RoPE on latent query–key blocks; a C2 projection keeps ECD peak counts and positions mirror-even and peak signs mirror-odd. GSF-χ leads every central-ECD output and improves axial Rotation and Symbol by 12.6% and 7.9% over the strongest baseline.
