Jiaqing Xie is a PhD student in Computer Science at [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn/). His research interests include spectral methods, graph theory, approximation theory, and AI for Science.

He received his M.Sc. in Computer Science from [ETH Zurich](https://ethz.ch/en.html), where he followed the Theoretical Computer Science track. Before that, he received his B.Eng. degree from the [University of Edinburgh](https://www.ed.ac.uk/).
